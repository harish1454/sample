# Vemana Poem

## Upamana Mudra (ఉపమాన ముద్ర)

అల్పుడెపుడు పల్కు నాడంబరముగాను
సజ్జనుండు పల్కు చల్లగాను
కంచు మ్రోగినట్లు కనకంబు మ్రోగునా
విశ్వదాభిరామ వినుర వేమ

### Translation

A mean person always speaks with pomp and show,
A noble person speaks with calm and cool,
Does gold resound the way bronze does?
Listen, Vema, beloved of Vishwadha.

---

### About Vemana

Vemana (c. 17th century) was a Telugu poet and philosopher known for his simple yet profound verses (*padyalu*). His poems cover themes of morality, spirituality, and social criticism, and remain widely quoted in Telugu-speaking regions.
