# Lion and Elephant Joke

**Why don't elephants ever challenge lions to a game of hide and seek?**

Because the lion always says, "Ready or not, here I *roam*!" ... and the elephant is way too big to hide behind a tree anyway.

---

**What happened when the lion tried to eat the elephant?**

He bit off more than he could chew!

---

**Why did the elephant refuse to fight the lion?**

He didn't want to be the elephant in the room at the lion's dinner table.
