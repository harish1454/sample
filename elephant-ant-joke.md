# Elephant and Ant Joke

**Why did the elephant sit on the ant?**

Because it wanted to be an *ant-crusher*... but the ant looked up and said, "Hey buddy, I've got family down here!"

---

**Why don't elephants ever play hide and seek with ants?**

Because the ants always win — they're too good at hiding in tiny spaces, and the elephants are terrible at fitting behind lamp posts! 🐘🐜
